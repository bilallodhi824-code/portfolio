GENEVA HOROLOGY

Uncompromising Swiss Precision

Every Watchify timepiece undergoes over 400 hours of meticulous assembly by Geneva's most accomplished master artisans. From heat-blued screws to hand-beveled tourbillon cages, perfection is our baseline standard.

  • Self-winding mechanical caliber with 28,800 vibrations per hour (4Hz).
  • Anti-magnetic silicon hairspring with 72-hour power reserve.
  • Skeletonized mainplate featuring hand-polished Côtes de Genève finish.
  • Solid 18K 750 gold, platinum 950, or aerospace-grade Grade 5 Titanium.
  • Double glareproofed sapphire crystals resistant to 9 Mohs hardness.
  • Ethically sourced alligator and calfskin leathers with deployment clasp.
  • COSC Certified Chronometer testing across 5 different positions.
  • 100m to 300m hydrostatic pressure testing in hyperbaric chambers.
  • Extreme thermal shock resilience from -20°C to +60°C.
